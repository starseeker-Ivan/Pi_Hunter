How it works. Digits of π are generated on this page by a verified continued-fraction spigot running in a background worker — no server, no precomputed table. The search reads the stream as it is produced and stops the moment your phrase appears.

Odds (rough). Assuming the digits behave like random strings, a random L-digit phrase first appears around 10^L digits: 3–4 digit phrases usually land inside the small caps; 5-digit phrases sometimes need the 50k–200k hunts; 6+ digits will almost never appear in these ranges. Letters become longer digit strings, so short words are the realistic targets.
